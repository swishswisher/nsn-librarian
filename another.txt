Title: Sourdough Bread Notes

Good sourdough bread depends on fermentation, hydration, and temperature. The starter should be active before mixing the dough, and gentle folding during fermentation helps strengthen the gluten. Baking in a preheated Dutch oven creates steam, producing a crisp crust and an airy interior.
